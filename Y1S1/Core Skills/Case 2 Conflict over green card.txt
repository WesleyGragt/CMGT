Case 2: Conflict over green card
Scenario: Engineer is saying the art is less valuable to the artist.

DESC:
	1. Analyse situation
	2. how you feel about it
	3. say what you would like to happen
	4. elaborate on the consequences.

SSC: 
	1. Is stopping a behaviour your doing
	2. start doing a behaviour 
	3. continue doing a behaviour.

Roleplay:
Receiver: Your art is worth nothing unlike my code, so I deserve the card more!

G: SSC
Hey, I noticed you undervalued the artists work.
Could you stop doing that and value it atleast a bit?
Other than that you are doing a good job on your side of the project!

G: DESC
Hey, I'm noticing you aren't appreciating the work the artist put into this project.
This hurts their feelings, which hurts the team spirit.
I'd like you to show more appreciation for their work,
so we can create a productive and encouraging work environment



SCC:
Engineer should stop undervalueing the artist contributions.
Start appreciating others work more.
Continue doing a good job towards the engineers side.

PRO: Actions are clear.
CON: Doesnt highlight the WHY.

Engineer doesnt believe artist deserves the green card and undervalues them using statements.
This can hurt the teamwork by hurting eachothers feelings.
Engineer should start valueing peoples work more.
Teamwork can go up and keep us more motivated.
