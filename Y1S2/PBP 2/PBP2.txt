I visited Hardt together with Anthonie, Demi, Kevin, Ilana and Guus. Hardt is located in Hengelo and is a creative agency in communication concepts, identities and high-profile campaigns.
They work with other brands like Thuisbezorgd.nl, Excite(previously Sigmax), Benerink and more.
I went with this group since I couldn't find a place to visit and the place had some big projects they had made with even bigger clients which looked interesting.
When I went there I hoped to learn a bit more about branding and showing appearance in a business field of view.

When we went to the place, we were told that the building itself used to be a old factory which was converted into workspaces and offices and is in the area where the hippiemoord happened.
The inside itself was a large open area with a small backyard. They have 3 long tables at the ground floor with a couple computers on top of them, a office where they can talk to clients and a breakroom.
You can also see the smaller 2nd floor at the top where there is another desk with a person working there. You can easily see whats happening on both floors at the same time.
The team itself consisted of 8 people including 2 designers, 2 webdesigners, a accountant and the owner himself. The owner himself talked in a informal way towards us and to his coworkers while we were there.
On a work day it Always starts at 8:30 and ends at 5pm with possible meetings throughout the week with clients.
They also start with a meeting on monday to check on progress and what will be done that week.
The projects themselves are made by having the client first talk about what they want and figure out what they need. Then it will be cut into smaller tasks and be given to the right person to be worked on with a deadline attached for the given task.

We had only spoken to Cliff while we did the visit.
He dropped out of school and did a lot of skating, but always liked design. One day he decided to get his own creative studio.
Which came with some challenges like owning any business has. He found it that it could be very stressfull, especially because he always wants the best for everyone.
There were 2 things he found very important, communication and doing it. That was also his advice for us. Communicate clearly to others, especially mistakes as they are human. And if you are considering doing something, just do it. The worst thing you can do is not doing it.
He did have a very good point with this since we are still young we can learn a lot of things that we could use and gain more knowledge in before having to actually put it in use for a higher level.

Before I went there i thought it would just be a simple way to get ideas for branding and identity.
While there it felt more like a friendly place with a open area with a few people that knew their fields well.
at the end of the trip I felt like i did learn a bit about how to do branding and identity but not as much as I wouldve liked.
The owner did give us good advice for how to communicate and to do it now instead of waiting for a bit.
I do think that the "do it now" part is definitely something I need since I like to put things off till a later time.

In the end I did see that working inside the creative business in the front face could be easy but in the background could still have some difficulties around. I did like the atmosphere that this small studio had and would like to work at a smaller studio myself when I try and get a job inside the creative industry. Althought not in the design part but definitely in the programming ways.
